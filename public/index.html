Welcome to Laya (लय)

🌊

1. Natural Cadence, Zero Broken Streaks

Traditional habit apps punish you with fiery streaks that shatter to zero if you catch the flu or take an intentional rest day.

Laya rejects streak anxiety. Consistency is measured as a continuous rhythm over time.

🎨

2. 3 Multi-Flavor Task Types

Not everything is a binary yes/no checkbox. Laya gives you:

  • Discrete Milestones: Track checkpoints like Drink 3L Water (1L, 2L, 3L).
  • Continuous Percentage: Track partial progress on books, projects, or sprints.
  • Standard Checklist: Clean binary checkoffs for workouts and habits.
🌙

3. The Guilt-Free "Ignore" Action

When life happens, tap the Rest / Ignore button.

Ignored tasks are subtracted from the denominator: $$\text{Consistency} = \frac{\text{Completed}}{\text{Scheduled} - \text{Ignored}} \times 100$$

Your score is preserved, your rest is honored, and you stay in rhythm guilt-free.
